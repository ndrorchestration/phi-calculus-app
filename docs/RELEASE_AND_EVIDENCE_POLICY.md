# Release and Evidence Policy

## Current posture

Phi-Calculus App is an experimental visualization/computational artifact. Project-local mathematical definitions, recurrence rules, visualizations, and computations must remain distinct from claims that a new mathematical theory has been independently established.

## Versioning

Use `0.x.y` while definitions, computational interfaces, and experimental interpretations remain under active development. A major version should represent a stable software contract, not a claim of mathematical proof.

## Evidence rule

A correct computation demonstrates the computation performed under the supplied definitions. It does not by itself establish the truth, novelty, or external validity of those definitions.

Formal claims require explicit derivation; empirical claims require reproducible experiments; external mathematical claims require appropriate authoritative sources.
