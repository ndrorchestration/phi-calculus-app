# Phi-Calculus App — Release and Evidence Policy

## Current status

**Active internal experimental research tool.** No project version or deployment state should be interpreted as mathematical validation.

## Evidence ladder

`DEFINED → COMPUTED → TESTED → NUMERICALLY VALIDATED → FORMALLY DERIVED → INDEPENDENTLY REPRODUCED`

These states are capability-specific. A visualization can be implemented while the mathematical proposition represented by that visualization remains unvalidated.

## Mathematical boundaries

The project-defined terms `phi-calculus`, `phi-weighted geometry`, `hendecabonacci experiments`, and related harmonic/modal constructs are research vocabulary unless a specific result is derived and supported.

The 11th-order recurrence is a valid defined recurrence. That does not establish that it is an oscillation kernel, optimal architecture, physical model, or universal mathematical law.

Likewise, a phi-weighted geometric visualization does not establish a result in standard Riemannian geometry, optimization, physics, or signal theory without the relevant derivation and evidence.

## Computational evidence

Executable calculations and CI tests establish behavior of the implementation under tested conditions. They do not independently prove the mathematical interpretation of the model.

## Promotion gates

Before making stronger mathematical claims, the repository should provide:

1. explicit definitions and notation;
2. derivation or proof for the claimed proposition;
3. executable numerical checks where appropriate;
4. reproducible test cases and tolerances;
5. comparison against relevant established baselines;
6. independent or repeated reproduction for consequential claims.

A future software release can change application interfaces without changing the evidence status of the underlying mathematical claims.
