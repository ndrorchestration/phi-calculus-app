# Contributing to Phi-Calculus-App

## Governance and evidence boundary

This repository is the **phi-harmonic calculus explorer** for related PhiLattice / PDMAL research. DGAF refers to the **Dynamic Governance Agentic Formation** framework and is a related governance/evaluation research dependency and lineage reference; it does not transfer mathematical validity, scientific state, certification, compliance, production readiness, or approval authority into this repository.

Current repository authority is project-local source, tests, documented evidence, and explicit human-maintainer decisions. Historical ecosystem personas such as **Agent Amethyst** and **Professor Prodigy** are preserved as lineage vocabulary where relevant; they are not current autonomous owners, approvers, or sign-off authorities.

> This is a **public repository**. Do not add private, secret, credential, or restricted source material. Historical NDR-133 references describe project provenance and do not make public repository contents private.

## How to Contribute

1. Open an issue with the proposed mathematical, implementation, documentation, or evidence change.
2. New mathematical claims must include a derivation or source that a human maintainer can review; empirical claims require claim-specific empirical evidence.
3. Visualizations must identify their mathematical or data source and distinguish modeled output from validated observation.
4. Submit a pull request with reproducible evidence appropriate to the claim and obtain explicit human-maintainer review before acceptance.
5. Preserve historical persona attribution when it is relevant provenance, but do not use persona identity as current approval authority.
